Announcing Cafe Kinship

Catch live shows on three festival stages, make beats with friends in Kinship Studios, hang out in the VIP lounge and shop the Sky Mall. Sign in with your Cafe Kinship account and your friends, artist status and beats come with you.

It's coming soon to the Epic Games Store for PC.
